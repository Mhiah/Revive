Community takeover, onchain.

A memecoin launchpad on NEAR where the dev has to stay alive, or the holders take over.

The problem

Most memecoins die the same way.

The dev goes quiet.
Holders are left with a coin nobody runs.
A "community takeover" today starts over off-chain, with no treasury and no claim on the dev's tokens.
How Revive works

Three rules, written into every coin.

♥Pulse

The dev sends a heartbeat on-chain before every deadline.

✕Flatline

Miss a deadline and the coin flatlines. Anyone can see it.

↻Takeover

Holders from before the death vote a Sputnik DAO in. The dev's locked tokens burn, the treasury moves, the coin gains a life.

Why you can trust it

Nothing to take on trust.

  • No keys on any coin or factory. The rules can't change.
  • Every coin's code, treasury and supply are checked live on its Verify tab.
  • 162 tests, including attacks and random 600-step histories.
  • A 60-day simulation on the real contract picked the default settings.

Dead coins can come back.
