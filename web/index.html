Lore — every death and revival, on-chain
    How it works

    The keeper must pulse on-chain before every deadline. Miss it and the coin flatlines. Holders who held before the flatline can vote a Sputnik DAO in as the new keeper. Tokens bought after the death count for nothing. If the vote passes, the creator's locked tokens burn, the treasury and links go to the DAO, and the coin gains a life. A late keeper can come back during the vote, but pays a penalty into the treasury.
